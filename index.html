Context

This was the first thing addressed in the audit — before touching anything else, the landing page made no attempt to show what the product actually does. The hero was purely text, and the studio link sat next to the primary signup button with no language distinguishing it: "Try the Studio" could easily have read as another path into signup rather than a no-commitment demo. The redesign replaces the hero with an interactive demo of all four tools on a real photo, and renames and repositions the studio link so it's unambiguous — "Explore the Studio Demo," placed directly beneath the proof it's pointing to.
